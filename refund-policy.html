Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Service Fulfillment

Our consulting fees compensate our case managers for the countless hours spent gathering, verifying, translating, modeling, and legally structuring your portfolio for execution. As such, once work has been initiated (e.g. your blueprint is generated and your documents have begun reviews), standard processing fees are non-refundable.

2. Visa Denials

Should your application be rejected by the corresponding immigration authority, Skats Consults does not offer refunds based on denial. As iterated in our Disclaimer, embassy decisions are fully sovereign and outside agency control. For denied clients, we offer an investigative refiling review at a significantly discounted procedural bracket.

3. Cancellations & Forfeiture

If a client decides to voluntarily abandon their case post-onboarding, any upfront deposits fall under forfeit. We understand that global circumstances change, and we will securely archive your generated file for 12 months in the event you restart processing.

4. Exception Cases

If an irrecoverable filing error is provably determined to have been committed by Skats Consuls causing an automatic administrative rejection, a case review will be opened by Senior Management yielding up to a 100% refund of consulting fees paid (excluding 3rd-party/government surcharges).
