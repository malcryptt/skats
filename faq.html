No. As stated in our Legal Disclaimers, no private agency can legally guarantee a visa. Embassy decisions are strictly sovereign. What we guarantee is a flawless, impeccably structured application that maximizes your chances of approval and avoids fatal technical errors.

Agency processing usually takes 5 - 14 business days to compile, audit, and route your file. Embassy processing times vary wildly. UK Priority might take 5 days, whereas US B1/B2 wait times could span several months depending on embassy backlogs.

Financial Proofs

Yes, provided the sponsor is a direct relative (parent, spouse, or sibling). You must establish a clear line of relation via birth/marriage certificates, alongside an official affidavit of support and 6 months of the sponsor’s bank statements demonstrating their source of funds.

Funds dumping is the sudden deposit of large unexplainable cash sums into an account right before printing a statement. Consular officers despise this. If large deposits are made, they must be backed by a clear paper trail (e.g., property sale deed, documented loan issuance).
