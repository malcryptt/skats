Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Skats Leadership Development Consults Ltd, we collect personal identification information including but not limited to your name, contact email, phone number, passport details, travel history, and financial standing whenever you submit an intake assessment or application processing request. This information is strictly mandated by international embassy processing standards.

2. Document Protection & Handling

Documents loaded to our Client Portal are stored on secure, encrypted servers. Data is utilized exclusively for evaluating visa eligibility and filing applications with the respective immigration authorities. Skats does not sell, trade, or otherwise rent your private credentials to any third party entities under any circumstance.

3. Communication Policy

By registering on our forms, you agree to receive essential email and WhatsApp correspondence from your dedicated Case Manager directly related to the statuses of your visa processing queues. Marketing and newsletter opt-ins are strictly voluntary and may be unsubscribed from unconditionally at any point.

4. Government and Legal Processing Disclosures

In accordance with global international immigration regulations, we are legally required to provide requested dossiers straight to government authorities when filing a case on your behalf. Any falsification or non-disclosure found in provided documentation does not hold Skats Consults liable.
