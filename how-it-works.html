Home How It Works
01

Strategic Initialization

Timeframe: 24-48 Hours

A dedicated Case Manager analyzes your professional footprint, travel history, and desired jurisdiction to draft an airtight procedural blueprint.

02

Documentation Assembly

Timeframe: 1-3 Weeks

We construct a secure file for you on our Portal. All documents are vetted individually against Embassy standards for alignment. Notarization and legal framing are applied here.

03

Diplomatic Submission

Timeframe: Same Day

The completed package is executed and transferred securely. We schedule your biometrics (if applicable) and monitor digital queues across embassy systems.

04

Approval & Briefing

Timeframe: Standard Embassy Queues

Upon successful acquisition of the visa, we conduct an exit-briefing—delivering your documentation alongside instructions for safe entry and local acclimation.
